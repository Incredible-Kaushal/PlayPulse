About Volleyball

Volleyball is a team sport played on a court divided by a net. Two teams try to send the ball over the net and make it land on the other side.

Players use passing, setting and attacking to keep the ball in play. Good communication between teammates is very important.

A team normally has six players on the court. Players rotate positions as the game continues.
