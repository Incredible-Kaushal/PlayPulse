About Basketball

Basketball is a team sport played between two teams. Players try to score points by putting the ball through the basket of the other team.

The game requires speed, good ball control and teamwork. Players pass and dribble the ball while trying to create scoring opportunities.

Basketball is played on a court with two baskets placed at opposite ends. The team with more points wins the game.
