About Football

Football is a team sport played between two teams of eleven players. The main aim of the game is to score more goals than the other team.

Players use passing, dribbling and shooting to move the ball around the field. Teamwork and communication are also important during a match.

A normal football match lasts for 90 minutes. It has two halves of 45 minutes each. The team with the most goals at the end of the match wins.
